# A Contract for Unfold and Fold

### Toward a Unified Operational Semantics across the Geometry of Refusal, the Harmogics Phases, and the Republic of Questions

*A working draft for system construction. CoAgnes internal document.*

---

## Preamble

This document specifies an operational contract between the two primitive operations of attention articulated within the CoAgnes corpus — *unfold* and *fold* — and the three frameworks that have so far described their ethical, phenomenological, and political conditions: the Geometry of Refusal, the seven Harmogics phases, and the Republic of Questions. The contract is intended as a draft foundation for the construction of executable systems in which directed attention must be structurally accountable: educational environments concerning AI ethics and regulatory practice, semantic publishing instruments, and decision-support architectures in regulated domains.

The contract holds that *unfold* and *fold* are not two distinct operations of equal type but a single asymmetric primitive of attention, and that the existing CoAgnes frameworks — formerly articulated in parallel — are in fact three views of this same primitive: its ethical discipline (Geometry of Refusal), its phenomenological grammar (Harmogics), and its political condition (Republic of Questions). What follows establishes the formal terms of this synthesis and derives from it the architectural requirements for any system that purports to instantiate it.

---

## I. The Two Primitive Operations

### 1.1 Definitions

**Unfold** is the operation by which a region of the semantic field, previously held as a unitary token of attention, is opened into the plurality of its constituent points. Unfold is a *temporal process*: it is realised as a sequence of acts of looking, each act selecting the next point of attention conditioned on the points already attended. Unfold is therefore inseparable from its *trace* — the ordered series of points opened — and the trace is itself a carrier of meaning irreducible to the set of opened points considered as an unordered collection.

**Fold** is the operation by which a region of attention, previously open in its plurality, is closed into a singular commitment that demarcates what is retained from what is released. Fold is *eventive*: it occurs at a moment, not over a duration, and produces a record (a *statement*) that thereafter participates in the substrate as an immutable artefact attributable to a named witness.

### 1.2 Asymmetry

Unfold and fold are not symmetric inverses. Their asymmetry has four structural components and is the source of their ethical and architectural significance.

*First*, in temporal profile: unfold is a process with internal history; fold is an instantaneous transition between states. The history of an unfold is not recoverable from its endpoint; the endpoint of a fold does not carry the history of its formation unless that history is independently preserved.

*Second*, in epistemic register: unfold answers the question *what is here to be seen*; fold answers the question *what shall be carried forward*. The first is an act of perception under discipline; the second is an act of judgement under accountability.

*Third*, in agential structure: unfold is distributable — multiple agents may share an unfold of the same region without contradiction. Fold is non-distributable — it is signed, dated, and attributed to a witness, and two witnesses producing distinct folds of the same unfold do not produce a single fold but two.

*Fourth*, in reversibility: an unfold may be re-entered, expanded, abandoned, or repeated without lasting consequence to the substrate. A fold, once committed, becomes part of the substrate and cannot be retracted; it can only be supplemented by subsequent folds that comment upon, qualify, or supersede it without erasing it.

### 1.3 The Composite Cycle

Within any disciplined act of attention there is a minimal composite cycle: an unfold in which a region of substrate is opened to its constituent plurality, followed by a fold in which a statement is committed that demarcates what the witness takes to be carried forward. This cycle is the elementary unit of semantic work. The frame, in the educational architecture, is its visible instantiation; the specimen, in the publishing architecture, is its archival form.

The quality of a composite cycle is characterised by three measurable properties:

1. **Breadth of unfold** — the cardinality of points attended within the region.
2. **Selectivity of fold** — the ratio between points retained in the statement and points opened in the unfold.
3. **Coherence between unfold and fold** — the degree to which the statement is structurally informed by the points opened, as opposed to being formulated independently of the unfold or in disregard of it.

A disciplined cycle exhibits sufficient breadth, principled selectivity, and high coherence. The failure modes are themselves diagnostic: low breadth indicates premature fold; low selectivity indicates fold-as-summary, that is, refusal to commit; low coherence indicates that the unfold was performative rather than constitutive of the fold.

## II. The Contract within the Geometry of Refusal

The Geometry of Refusal articulates three layers at which a system may withhold action: the legal layer of jurisdictional constraint, the layer of semantic invariants where structural inversion is detected, and the layer of geometric computation where context-specific judgement is exercised. This architecture is here re-stated as a phased application of unfold and fold under conditions of refusal, with the irreducibility of the third layer to algorithmic decision preserved as its constitutive principle.

### 2.1 Layer I — Legal Constraint

At the legal layer, unfold is reduced to a *lookup*: the system unfolds the requested action against a registry of jurisdictional prohibitions, and the operation terminates either in admissibility or in the immediate fold of refusal. The fold here is non-deliberative; its statement is a citation. The operation is the canonical Vessel `stop`, here re-described as the limit case of the cycle in which the unfold is bounded to a single test and the fold contains no semantic surplus.

### 2.2 Layer II — Semantic Invariants

At the layer of semantic invariants, unfold is *structural*: the system opens the request into its semantic geometry and tests whether the spectrum of attended points collapses under increase of resolution. A request that survives unfold preserves its plurality; a request that does not survive unfold reveals its character as a mirage — a verbal construction whose interior is empty or whose surface contradicts its claimed substance. The fold at this layer is *negative*: it is a fold of refusal grounded not in jurisdictional constraint but in structural vacuity. The statement of refusal records what was unfolded and what failed to be present.

This layer is the proper home of the contract's diagnostic function. The detection of mirage is not the application of a rule but the conduct of a disciplined unfold whose result is the recognition of absence. The discipline cannot be replaced by a classifier; it can only be supported by one.

### 2.3 Layer III — Geometric Computation

At the layer of geometric computation, unfold is *coordinate-producing*: the system opens the situation into the three coordinates of position (the vector of power within which the actor stands), yield (whether the proposed action increases or decreases the visibility of the situation to those affected by it), and frame (the larger system within which the action will be received). The fold at this layer is *honest geometry*: it commits the witness to the rendering of these coordinates, but does not commit the witness to a verdict.

This is the layer at which the contract is most strict, and most demanding of the human witness. The fold here is irreducibly the human's act. The system may unfold; the system may render; the system may even propose; but the fold of judgement at the geometric layer is non-delegable, by architectural requirement. *CoAgnes computes coordinates and renders geometry. It does not issue moral verdicts.*

### 2.4 The Layered Contract

The three layers stand in a strict order of application: a request is admissible at Layer I before it is unfolded at Layer II, and survives unfolding at Layer II before it reaches Layer III. The fold at each layer is conclusive for that layer; the cycle proceeds to the next only if the fold is not one of refusal. This produces a clean operational sequence — *test, unfold, render, witness* — that may be implemented without ambiguity and audited without interpretive labour.

## III. The Contract within the Harmogics Phases

The Harmogics framework names seven phases of witnessing: Truth, Deep, Connect, Service, Knowledge, Evolution, Responsibility. The contract holds that these phases are not seven types of inquiry but seven *modes of unfold* — seven structurally distinct ways in which a region of substrate may be opened to its plurality — together with the corresponding modes of fold by which each may be brought to commitment. The phases discipline the unfold by typing the kind of point that may be attended within them; they discipline the fold by typing the kind of statement that may issue from them.

### 3.1 The Phases as Unfold Modes

| Phase | Unfold attends to | Fold commits to |
|---|---|---|
| **Truth** | Points of established fact, verifiable at primary source | A statement of what is the case |
| **Deep** | Points of structural ground, the substrate conditions of appearance | A statement of what underlies |
| **Connect** | Points of relational pattern, the topology binding phenomena | A statement of what relates to what |
| **Service** | Points of contextual purpose, the for-whom of activity | A statement of whom the act answers to |
| **Knowledge** | Points of received understanding, the inheritance preceding the inquiry | A statement of what has been learned and is now applied |
| **Evolution** | Points of trajectory, the directionality through time | A statement of what is becoming |
| **Responsibility** | Points of attributed agency, the structure of answerability | A statement of who stands behind the commitment |

A composite cycle that operates in only one phase produces an *atomised statement* — a fact, a relation, a name — adequate only when the situation is itself reducible to that phase. A composite cycle that operates across several phases produces a *coherent witnessing* — a quantity held in multiple registers simultaneously, whose force is greater than the sum of its phase-restricted components.

### 3.2 The Ethics of Unfold Sequencing

The ordering of phases within an unfold is not arbitrary; it is itself the carrier of meaning. The sequence *Truth → Deep → Connect → Service → Knowledge → Evolution → Responsibility* is the canonical *forward order* of inquiry: from what is, to what underlies, to what connects, to what is for, to what is known, to what is becoming, to who answers. The reverse order — beginning from Responsibility and reading backward through Evolution toward Truth — is the canonical *governance order*: the order proper to retrospective accountability and to the evaluation of completed acts.

A trace that proceeds in forward order is engaged in *constitutive inquiry*: it builds the situation. A trace that proceeds in reverse order is engaged in *retrospective examination*: it tests the situation already built. Other orderings are possible and meaningful, but the forward and reverse orders are the two canonical disciplines, and the system must record which discipline a given trace exhibits.

The ethical content of this sequencing is precise: *to unfold prematurely into Responsibility before having unfolded Truth is to attribute agency to an unestablished fact*. To unfold into Service before having unfolded Connect is to claim purpose without relational ground. The phases discipline each other through their order, and the discipline cannot be enforced from outside the trace; it must be exhibited by the trace itself.

### 3.3 Phase-Balanced Fold

A fold is *phase-balanced* when its statement carries the phases that were unfolded in proportion to their weight in the unfold. A fold that is unbalanced — that retains only the Truth-phase points of an unfold that traversed all seven phases, or that retains the Connect and Service phases while abandoning the Truth that grounded them — is a fold whose statement misrepresents its own conditions. Phase imbalance in fold is one of the principal failure modes the system is built to detect, render visible, and discourage.

## IV. The Contract within the Republic of Questions

The Republic of Questions articulates the political condition under which unfold and fold can operate with integrity: the condition of attentive presence, in which questions are sovereign, the labour of formulation is honoured, and the act of concealment is named. The contract holds that unfold and fold are not merely cognitive operations but political ones, and that their integrity depends on conditions that the system must structurally maintain.

### 4.1 Attentive Presence as Operational Condition

Attentive presence is the condition under which unfold can be honest and fold can be accountable. It is not a state of the witness but a configuration of the system: the substrate is preserved against drift, the trace is preserved against erasure, the statement is preserved against silent revision. Without these preservations, unfold becomes performance and fold becomes pretence.

Operationally, attentive presence requires three structural guarantees:

1. **Substrate immutability** — the points opened in past unfolds, and the statements committed in past folds, remain available to any subsequent unfold. The history of the field is a property of the field.
2. **Trace integrity** — the order in which points were opened and the temporal intervals between openings are preserved, because the trace carries meaning irreducible to the set of opened points.
3. **Witness attribution** — each fold is signed, and the signature is non-removable. To remove a signature is itself a recorded act, attributable to a named agent.

### 4.2 The Sovereignty of Questions

In the Republic, the question is sovereign and the answer is provisional. Translated into the contract: the *unfold-question* — the question that opens a region of substrate to attention — has structural priority over the *fold-statement* that closes it. A statement that does not carry within it the questions it was formed to answer is a statement detached from its conditions; such a statement is fragile under any subsequent unfold that re-opens the questions it was meant to address.

This translates into an architectural requirement: every fold must preserve the *unfold-question lineage* that produced it. The statement is not the answer alone but the answer-with-its-questions, and the lineage is part of the substrate.

### 4.3 The Naming of Concealment

In the Republic, to hide a question is a recorded act, attributable, dated, permanent. Translated into the contract: the *non-unfolding* of a region available for unfold is itself a trace event. A region that was structurally available for attention, and that the witness chose not to attend, is recorded as a *negative point* in the trace — not as a failure, not as a fault, but as a structural fact about what the witness elected not to see.

This is the architectural counterpart to the political principle: *the act of looking away has the same accountability as the act of looking*. Systems that record only what was attended, and not what was structurally available but not attended, produce traces that misrepresent the witness's actual posture toward the substrate.

## V. Operational Specifications

The synthesis above produces the following requirements for any system that purports to instantiate the contract.

### 5.1 Substrate

The substrate is the persistent semantic field shared across all frames of all stories of all witnesses. It contains:

- **Points** — individual semantic elements, typed by Harmogics phase and attributed to the unfolds and folds that established them.
- **Statements** — committed folds, each signed, dated, and lineage-bearing.
- **Traces** — ordered sequences of unfold acts, each preserving order and inter-event intervals.
- **Negative-points** — regions structurally available for unfold but not attended, recorded with the trace in which the non-attendance occurred.

The substrate is append-only at the level of folds and traces. Points may be enriched but not removed.

### 5.2 Frame

A frame is a bounded region of the substrate, scoped by composition of *aspects* (Harmogics phases active within it, regulatory domains in scope, narrative coordinates of the situation), within which a witness conducts a composite unfold-fold cycle. The frame carries:

- a *bootstrap state* — the minimal points fixed before any unfold by the witness begins (who is present, where, when, what is in tension);
- an *unfold space* — the set of points structurally available for attendance;
- a *trace under construction* — the witness's ordered acts of attendance;
- a *statement under formation* — the fold being assembled.

The frame closes when the statement is committed. Subsequent frames inherit from the closed frame's substrate contribution but begin their own unfold from their own bootstrap.

### 5.3 Reflective Stream

A parallel non-generative process maintains the integrity of the substrate during live composition. Its function is *fixation*: extraction of entities, relations, and contradictions as they emerge in the unfold-fold cycle, and propagation of these fixations as constraints on subsequent frame generation. The reflective stream is implemented partly through deterministic structural extraction and partly through generative inference; the two are kept architecturally distinct so that their failure modes are independently auditable.

### 5.4 Quality Metrics

The system computes, for each composite cycle and for each witness's history of cycles:

- *unfold breadth* — the cardinality of attended points;
- *unfold phase distribution* — the spread across Harmogics phases;
- *unfold sequence character* — forward, reverse, or other discipline;
- *fold selectivity* — the retention ratio between unfold and statement;
- *fold phase balance* — the proportional preservation of phases across the cycle;
- *unfold-fold coherence* — the structural informedness of the statement by the unfold;
- *negative-point density* — the proportion of available unfold space not attended.

These metrics are not evaluative in the sense of producing a score; they are *descriptive of the witness's posture*, and they enter the final phase analysis as the substrate of self-understanding.

### 5.5 Final Phase Analysis

At the close of a story (a sequence of frames), the system conducts a phase analysis of the entire trace: the seven Harmogics phases are applied as lenses to the accumulated unfolds and folds, and the result is a structured rendering of the witness's trajectory. This analysis is itself an unfold — a phase-disciplined opening of the trace as substrate — and produces a fold of its own, which becomes a substrate point for any subsequent story by the same witness.

## VI. Closing Principle

The contract's closing principle restates and unifies the closing principles of its three constituent frameworks:

> *Unfold can be assisted, distributed, accelerated, and made more rigorous by the semantic engine. Fold cannot be delegated. The fold is the moment at which the witness commits, signs, and accepts that the substrate now contains a statement attributable to them. The architecture is built so that this moment cannot be obscured, automated, or absorbed by the machine.*

This is the architectural form of the Geometry of Refusal's closing principle ("CoAgnes computes coordinates and renders geometry; it does not issue moral verdicts"), of Harmogics' principle of Responsibility as the seventh and irreducible phase, and of the Republic's principle of named accountability for every act of inclusion and concealment. The three frameworks do not merely converge on this principle; they are three articulations of it.

The contract is therefore not a synthesis in the sense of an external structure imposed on three previously independent frameworks. It is the recognition that *the three frameworks were always describing the same thing*: the irreducibility of the human fold, considered respectively as ethical demand (Refusal), as phenomenological discipline (Harmogics), and as political condition (Republic). The unfold-fold pair is the operational primitive in which all three are instantiated.

A system constructed on this contract is not a tool for thought. It is a *disciplined environment for the conduct of attention*, in which the conduct itself is the deliverable, the trace is the artefact, and the witness remains the irreducible source of commitment.

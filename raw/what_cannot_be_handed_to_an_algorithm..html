01 Responsibility apex · the bearer

The one act AI cannot perform today: to answer for an outcome. A model can decide; only a bearer can be held to the decision. Whether that bearer could ever be non-human — perhaps paired with a person — is an open question this sheet does not pretend to close.

02 Intuition faculty · below the token

It lives beneath language — often not even verbal to the person who carries it. AI operates on the verbal layer; intuition is the floor under it. Not a weaker kind of reasoning — a different stratum.

03 The legally significant fold act · the binding

fold — the binding act after which something holds: a signature, a consent, a decision answered for. Possible only inside human responsibility, steered by intuition, grounded in a legal frame. AI may prepare it; only one who can answer may fold it.
